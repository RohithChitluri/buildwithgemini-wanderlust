# My agent: Wanderlust - Curated Travel & Day-Trip Planner
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps curious travelers discover and plan personalized day-trips with a curated catalog of destinations, activities, and scenic viewpoints.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Traveler profile (budget tier, pace preferences, accessibility needs, favorite cuisines, past visited destinations).
- Tools: Look up attractions/day-trips by vibe & budget, weather checks for trip dates, travel time/distance estimator.
- Catalog/UI: Curated day-trip destination cards (ratings, vibe tags, estimated cost, duration) and step-by-step itinerary timeline tables.
- Image gen: Generates a stylized scenic postcard or teaser photo of recommended destinations.
- Sandbox: Custom day-trip budget calculation and route pacing/time breakdown.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for multi-stop route optimization & budget math, Maps integration, exportable itinerary summaries.
